Product data in thai_products.tsv

Contains information from Open Food Facts (https://world.openfoodfacts.org), a free, community-built
database of food products. It is made available under the Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
The individual contents of the database are made available under the Database Contents License (DbCL) 1.0:
https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/

What this file is: a reduced extract of that database (products sold in Thailand, or with a barcode starting
with 885, the GS1 prefix for Thailand), keeping only the barcode, name, brand, serving size, calories, protein,
carbohydrate and fat per 100 g. It was produced by tools/build_thai_products.py from the public CSV export at
https://static.openfoodfacts.org/data/en.openfoodfacts.org.products.csv.gz

Under the ODbL this extract is itself available under the ODbL: you may copy, share and adapt it, as long as you
credit Open Food Facts, say if you changed it, and keep it under the same license.

The data is entered by volunteers and may contain mistakes or be out of date. Always check the label on the pack.
To add or correct a product, contribute at https://world.openfoodfacts.org
